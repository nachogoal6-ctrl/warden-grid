Use it well

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width.
  • Don't recolour the mark outside the palette, rotate it, stretch it, or add effects other than a green glow.
  • Green on near-black is the default. Use the one-colour mark on busy backgrounds.
  • Red is reserved for the Breach state.
